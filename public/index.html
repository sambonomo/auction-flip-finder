Paste or upload the auction list. A Manheim export file (Export.csv) loads as it is. Best columns: Auction, Lane, Run (or Lot / Work Order), Year, Make, Model, CR Grade, Adj MMR, MMR @ 4.0, Est Bid, Announcements. The real MMR @ 4.0 from the MMR tool matters most: without it the sell value is a guess. VIN is optional. Column order doesn't matter, and the app turns announcements into a repair estimate automatically.

Copy into Excel or Google Sheets, fill it in, save as CSV, then upload it.

How a car is scored

Repair cost is priced line by line from the announcements, using your price book (Settings). Each repainted panel lowers the grade the car can realistically reach, since AutoGrade deducts for previous repairs. The sell value is MMR at that reachable grade, using your “MMR @ 4.0” number when you have one or the % per grade point when you don't, then lowered for market drift over the days held. Costs include buyer fee, transport, recon, a surprise reserve, the exit's selling costs, holding cost and floor-plan interest. Max bid hits your target on paper; walk-away hits your floor; safe bid is the highest bid that still makes your floor in 7 of 10 replays. The stress test re-runs each car with recon over budget and a lower grade. Odds replays each flip 300 times with the things that go wrong (grade lands short, recon runs over, a no-sale, a soft sale price, a hidden problem) and shows how often it still makes your profit floor. A car that clears the target on paper but has under 50% odds is a Maybe, not a Buy. Tap any car to adjust its numbers.

Load sales history

Load the auction's sales history. The app finds cars that sold twice, bought rough and re-sold, and runs each pair through the same cost model. Recon isn't known, so each pair shows the most the buyer could have spent and still broken even, plus estimated profit using your recon $ per grade point.

One row per sale: Sale Date, Sale Price, Grade, Odometer, plus VIN if available. Add an Auction column (Dallas or DFW) to compare the two lanes. Without VINs it matches on Year / Make / Model / Trim / Color and mileage.

About the re-run and odds numbers

A re-run is assumed to sell at 98% of MMR with a 60% chance each run, and every car gets odds. These are estimates until real sales are loaded. To get the old numbers back, set “Re-run sells at” and “Chance it sells each run” to 100 and the no-sale fee to 0. The Garage and the Run List measure these numbers from real results and offer to apply them.

Buyer fee table

Used when Buyer fee is set to the tiered schedule. Manheim doesn't publish one national buyer fee schedule; each location sets its own. These rows are placeholders. Copy the real numbers from the Manheim Dallas and Dallas-Fort Worth fee sheets (they are in the Manheim account under each location). If the two lanes differ, enter the higher one.

Manheim Dallas (W Kiest Blvd) and Manheim Dallas-Fort Worth (Euless) are about 20 miles apart, so a local shuttle or driving the car costs far less than a long haul. Transport is set to $150 per trip (in and out) for local cars; put in what you actually pay.

Recon price book

What each repair costs at your shop. “Paint” items count as repainted panels, which lowers the grade a car can reach after repair.
